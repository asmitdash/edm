# New Employee Onboarding — Standard Operating Procedure

## Purpose
Ensure new hires reach productivity quickly with the right access, equipment, and context, while meeting compliance obligations.

## Scope
Applies to all full-time and contract hires.

## Roles & Responsibilities
- **People Operations:** Owns end-to-end onboarding plan.
- **Hiring Manager:** First-week plan, role-specific ramp-up.
- **IT / Workplace:** Device provisioning, account creation.
- **Buddy:** Peer assigned for the first 30 days.

## Trigger
- Signed offer letter received.

## Procedure Steps
1. Send pre-boarding email within 1 business day of signed offer.
2. Collect documentation: ID, tax forms, banking, emergency contact.
3. IT provisions laptop, email, SSO, role-specific tooling 3 days before start.
4. Hiring Manager prepares first-week plan and shares 1 day before start.
5. Day 1: orientation, IT walkthrough, badge/access cards, intro to Buddy.
6. Day 2-5: function-specific orientation, training modules.
7. Day 30: 30-day check-in with People Ops + Hiring Manager.
8. Day 90: confirm probation completion or extend if needed.

## Exceptions & Conditional Rules
- Remote hires receive equipment via approved courier; cannot start without confirmed receipt.
- Contractors skip benefits enrollment but follow same access provisioning.
- Hires for security-sensitive roles complete background check before access provisioning.
- International hires: visa/work authorization documents take precedence; legal review required before start.

## Escalation
- Documentation missing > 5 days before start → People Operations Lead.
- IT delay risking start date → IT Manager.

## Compliance Notes
- I-9 / right-to-work verification required before day 1.
- Data privacy training must be completed within first 7 days.
