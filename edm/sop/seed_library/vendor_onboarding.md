# Vendor Onboarding & Risk Review — Standard Operating Procedure

## Purpose
Onboard new vendors in a way that meets security, legal, and finance requirements before any payment or data sharing occurs.

## Scope
Applies to any third party that will receive payment, customer data, or system access.

## Roles & Responsibilities
- **Requesting Manager:** Sponsors the engagement, owns business case.
- **Procurement:** Coordinates intake, contract template selection.
- **Security:** Reviews data-handling and access scope.
- **Legal:** MSA / DPA review.
- **Finance:** Vendor master setup, payment terms.

## Procedure Steps
1. Manager files vendor intake request with use case, expected spend, data scope.
2. Procurement classifies risk tier (low / medium / high) by spend, data sensitivity, criticality.
3. Security questionnaire issued for medium / high tier vendors.
4. Legal reviews MSA + DPA. Negotiate redlines.
5. Finance creates vendor record with W-9 / W-8 documentation.
6. Manager confirms acceptance criteria for first deliverable.
7. Procurement issues PO once contract signed.
8. Security access granted only after contract execution.
9. Annual review scheduled for high-tier vendors.

## Exceptions & Conditional Rules
- Vendors with PII / PHI access require completed SIG questionnaire and SOC 2 report.
- Spend < $5k and no data access can use the lightweight intake form.
- Vendors HQ'd in restricted jurisdictions require export control review.
- Emergency procurement: 48-hour expedited path with retroactive Legal review allowed only with VP-level sign-off.

## Escalation
- High-risk security gaps unresolved → CISO.
- Legal redlines unresolved > 10 days → General Counsel.
- Spend exceeds approver authority → next level per delegation matrix.

## Compliance Notes
- DPA required for any vendor processing personal data.
- Anti-bribery / anti-corruption attestation required for international vendors.
- Vendor records retained 7 years post-termination.
