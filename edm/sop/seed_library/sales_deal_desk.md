# Sales Deal Desk Approval — Standard Operating Procedure

## Purpose
Govern non-standard pricing, contract terms, and commercial concessions to protect margin while enabling deal velocity.

## Scope
Applies to all opportunities with non-standard discount, contract length, payment terms, or legal redlines.

## Roles & Responsibilities
- **Account Executive:** Initiates desk request, owns customer communication.
- **Deal Desk Analyst:** Triages, validates pricing, builds approval package.
- **VP Sales:** Approves margin-impact decisions within thresholds.
- **CFO:** Approves above-threshold concessions.

## Trigger
- Discount > 15% off list.
- Multi-year contract with prepayment requested.
- Custom legal terms not in MSA template.
- Annual contract value > $250k.

## Procedure Steps
1. AE submits deal desk request in CRM with deal stage at "negotiation".
2. Analyst reviews within 1 business day.
3. Analyst validates pricing model and computes margin impact.
4. If within AE's authority, return with approval and required language.
5. If escalation needed, build approval package: pricing, term, justification, comparable deals.
6. Route to VP Sales for approval up to 25% discount or $500k ACV.
7. Route to CFO for higher concessions or non-standard payment terms.
8. Approval recorded in CRM with conditions.
9. Approved terms sent to AE; AE updates contract.
10. Legal review if any custom redlines present.

## Exceptions & Conditional Rules
- Discounts > 30% always require CFO approval regardless of ACV.
- Non-standard liability terms always route to Legal before approval.
- Quarter-end deals: 24-hour SLA on desk turnaround if filed by EOD T-3.
- Public sector / government deals follow separate SOP.

## Escalation
- Approval needed faster than SLA → Deal Desk Lead.
- Legal redlines unresolved at T-2 → VP Sales + General Counsel.

## Compliance Notes
- All approvals retained 7 years for audit.
- Revenue recognition implications must be flagged to Finance for any prepayment > $1M.
